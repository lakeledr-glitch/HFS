Getting to know Hilton Foundation Schools

A closer look at who we are, what we stand for, and the values that shape every classroom.

Our Story

Founded on a simple idea

A child's first school should feel like a safe place to try, fail, and try again.

Hilton Foundation Schools sits behind Essa Atagi Soro Estate, around the National Open University area in Offa, Kwara State — a school built for families who want more than a classroom seat for their child: a place that pays attention.

We keep our sections — Creche, Nursery and Primary — closely knit, so that no child passes through unnoticed and no parent is left guessing how their child is really doing.

Vision & Mission

What we're working toward

Our Vision — to raise confident, capable young learners who are well prepared for every stage that follows their time with us.

Our Mission — to provide a safe, structured and encouraging environment where every child's academic, social and personal foundation is built with intention.

  • Individual attention, even in a full classroom
  • Consistent communication with parents
  • Discipline balanced with warmth

Programme Stages

Three stages, closely connected

Each stage hands the next a child who's ready — academically and emotionally.

Stage 01 · Early Years

Creche & Daycare

Our youngest learners are held in a warm, secure setting focused on comfort, routine and gentle stimulation — feeding times, nap schedules and play are all structured around each child's needs.

Play-based learning Sensory activities Nap & feeding routine
Stage 02 · Pre-Primary

Nursery School

Children build early literacy and numeracy through phonics, songs, storytelling and hands-on activities. Social skills — sharing, taking turns, following instructions — are taught just as intentionally as letters and numbers.

Phonics & early reading Number work Social & motor skills
Stage 03 · Basic 1 – 6

Primary School

A full academic curriculum across core and creative subjects, paired with structured assessment, sports and character development — preparing pupils confidently for secondary education.

Core subjects Creative & arts Sports & clubs

Admission is open — here's how it works

A simple, guided process from your first enquiry to your child's first day.

The Process

Five steps to enrollment

1

Make an Enquiry

Call, WhatsApp or visit the school office to ask about space availability for your child's age group.

2

Tour the School

Come see the classrooms, meet staff and get a feel for the environment your child will be learning in.

3

Assessment / Interaction

A simple, age-appropriate interaction to help us place your child in the right class.

4

Submit Documentation

Complete the registration form and provide the required documents (see checklist below).

5

Confirm Enrollment

Complete registration formalities with the school office and receive your child's resumption details.

Before You Apply

What you'll need on hand

Registration Checklist
